Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 12, 2026

FatiWeb Market ("the app") is an Android marketplace application connecting customers and vendors. This policy describes the data we collect, why we collect it, and how you can exercise your rights.

1. Data we collect

CategoryDataPurpose
Account Name, email address, phone number, profile photo, Google identifier (if you use Google Sign-In) Account creation and management, authentication
Delivery Delivery addresses; approximate or precise device location (only if you grant permission, to pre-fill an address) Delivering your orders
Orders Order history, purchased items, amounts, delivery status Processing and tracking your purchases
Messages Customer–vendor conversations, messages exchanged with the assistant Buyer–seller communication, support
Photos Profile picture (avatar); product photos uploaded by vendors Profile personalization, product presentation
Notifications Push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) Informing you about order status and announcements
Diagnostics Anonymized usage data, crash reports, performance data (Google Analytics for Firebase, Crashlytics) Improving stability and experience

2. Payment

Payment is exclusively cash on delivery. The app does not collect, process, or store any payment card data or other payment information.

3. Hosting and processors

Data is stored and processed using Google Firebase services (Google LLC): Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions (europe-west1 region, Belgium), Firebase Cloud Messaging, Google Analytics for Firebase, and Crashlytics. All communications are encrypted in transit (TLS).

4. Data sharing

  • Vendors see the information needed to fulfill your orders (name, delivery address, phone, ordered items).
  • We do not sell any personal data to third parties.
  • No data is shared for advertising purposes.

5. Retention

Your data is retained while your account is active. When you delete your account, your personal data is removed; past orders are kept in anonymized form (personal data redacted) for vendors' bookkeeping purposes.

6. Your rights

At any time you can:

  • view and edit your information under Profile → Personal details;
  • manage notification preferences in the app settings;
  • delete your account and data directly in the app — see the Account deletion page;
  • contact us with any question or request regarding your data.

7. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data about them.

8. Contact

9. Changes

This policy may be updated; the last-updated date appears at the top of this page. Material changes will be announced in the app.
