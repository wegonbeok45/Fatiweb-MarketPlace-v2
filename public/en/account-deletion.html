Deleting your account and data

Last updated: June 12, 2026

You can delete your FatiWeb Market account and associated data at any time, directly from the app.

From the app

  1. Open the FatiWeb Market app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Tap Personal details.
  4. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Deletion is immediate and permanent.

What is deleted

  • Your account (sign-in credentials, email, phone, Google sign-in link);
  • your profile (name, profile photo, preferences);
  • your delivery addresses;
  • your cart, favorites, and saved searches;
  • your notification tokens;
  • your conversations (hidden on your side).

What is retained

  • Past orders are kept in anonymized form: your personal data (name, address, phone) is redacted from them. This allows vendors to keep an accounting record of their sales.

Without access to the app

If you can no longer access the app, send a deletion request by email to tata.gounounou@gmail.com from the email address associated with your account, with the subject "FatiWeb account deletion". Your request will be processed within 30 days.
